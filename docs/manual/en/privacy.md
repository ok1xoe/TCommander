# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

**TCommander does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to its developer or to anybody else.**

## What the app does with your data

- **Your files.** TCommander reads and changes only the files and folders you open in it, and only when you ask. File names, contents and metadata never leave your Mac, except to the servers you connect to yourself (see below).
- **Settings.** Your preferences, keyboard shortcuts, favorite folders and saved connections are stored locally on your Mac, in the app's support folder.
- **Passwords.** Passwords for saved connections are stored in your macOS Keychain, not in a file.
- **Network connections.** TCommander connects to a server only when you tell it to (FTP, FTPS, SMB, WebDAV, SFTP). The data go directly between your Mac and that server.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts and no sign-in.
- No analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.
- No tracking, and no data shared with third parties.
- No automatic connection to the internet. (The links in the Help menu open your web browser only when you choose them.)

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anybody.
